PUT YOUR NON-BANANA KML FILES HERE
===================================

These are polygons of OTHER crop types or land cover from ANY region.
The more diverse the regions, the better the model generalises.

Examples of what to put here:
  - Sugarcane fields (Maharashtra, UP)
  - Cotton fields (Vidarbha, Gujarat)
  - Paddy / rice fields (Tamil Nadu, West Bengal)
  - Orchards (mango, orange)
  - Fallow land
  - Urban / built-up areas
  - Water bodies

Naming convention:
  Include a date if available (same format as banana KMLs).
  Include a crop/region hint in the name:

  Examples:
    sugarcane_nashik_10jun2023.kml
    cotton_akola_5oct2023.kml
    paddy_tn_20aug2023.kml
    fallow_gujarat_1dec2023.kml

  If no date is in the filename, pass start_date and end_date explicitly
  when calling SampleGenerator.generate().

How many do you need?
  Aim for roughly the same total area as your banana KMLs.
  50-80 banana plots → 50-80 non-banana polygons from diverse regions.
  The system will automatically balance the class counts.
